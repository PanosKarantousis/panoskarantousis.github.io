Maevious Learn — Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Controller: Daralexion Ltd · Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Version 1.1

This policy sets out how long Daralexion Ltd keeps personal data collected through the Maevious Learn app, and how it is deleted. It satisfies the app stores' deletion requirements (including Apple's requirement for in-app account deletion) and the UK GDPR storage-limitation and right-to-erasure principles.

1. Principle

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed to provide the app to your family, and no longer. Because the app is built on data minimisation, there is very little to retain in the first place.

Learning content (lessons, questions) is not personal data. It is never hard-deleted; superseded content is retired so that offline caches learn to remove it (a design requirement of the sync model), not for any personal-data reason.

3. How deletion works

In-app account deletion (self-service)

  1. In the app: Settings → (adult check) → Delete account.
  2. The app calls our account-deletion server function, which:
    • verifies your identity from your own session token (it never trusts an account id supplied in the request);
    • deletes your authentication user with an administrative key server-side only;
    • the database then cascades the deletion down the ownership chain, removing every row belonging to your family — every learner profile, all progress and streaks, your question reports and concern messages, and your referral state; anyone you referred simply loses the link to your deleted account.
  3. The deletion is permanent and irreversible. This is covered by our automated database test suite, which asserts that deleting the account removes all descendant records.

On request

You may also ask us to erase your data by contacting info@maevious.com. We will verify you are the account holder and action it, normally within one month (UK GDPR).

4. Backups

Personal data may persist briefly in encrypted database backups after deletion. Backups roll off on our provider's schedule (typically a small number of days for the point-in-time window), after which the data is gone from backups too. Restored backups are re-subjected to any outstanding deletion requests.

5. Processor deletion

On termination of a processor relationship, or on instruction, our processors (currently Supabase and Resend) delete or return personal data per their data-processing terms.

6. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and whenever the data model changes.

Notes on children and special categories

  • No special-category data (Art. 9) is processed: no health, biometric, religious, political, or similar data. Year level is an educational filter, not a special category.
  • Children. Identifiable and consent-bearing data sits on the adult. The child's footprint is a nickname + level + cosmetic avatar + progress. Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) is deliberately not relied on for any child-facing processing; the basis is contract with the adult account holder. This avoids relying on a child's ability to consent (UK age of ISS consent is 13) and keeps marketing consent entirely on Maevious Ltd's website.
  • Marketing to children is never performed. Any marketing is adult-only, consent-based, and collected by the other controller.

Legitimate Interests Assessments (LIAs)

Short LIAs are held for items 6, 7, 9 and 10. Each concludes: the interest is legitimate (security / content quality / product improvement / optional growth), the processing is necessary and minimised, and it does not override the interests of parents or children because it involves no child PII, no profiling of children, no tracking SDKs, and EU hosting. Analytics can be operated without a child ever being identified.

Review

Reviewed whenever the data model or analytics event set changes, and at least annually.

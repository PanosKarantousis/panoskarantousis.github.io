Your privacy — the short version

Hi! This is Maevious Learn. This page explains what we know about you, in plain words. There's a longer version for your parent or carer too.

What we know about you

Not very much — on purpose.

  • A nickname — whatever name you or your grown-up picked for your profile.
  • Your curriculum and your year or grade — like "England, Year 8" or "United States, 6th grade" — so we can show you the right topics first. You can always practise easier or harder stuff too.
  • Your avatar — the little character and colour you chose. It's just for fun.
  • Your practice — which topics you've tried, your XP, and your streak, so we can show your progress and remember where you got to.

What we do NOT know

  • We do not know your real full name, your birthday, or where you live.
  • We do not have your email or phone, and there's no way for anyone to message you in the app. No chat, ever.
  • We do not show you adverts, and we don't follow you around the internet.

Who set this up

A grown-up set up the account — that's your parent or carer. They can see your progress, and they can delete everything whenever they want.

Your grown-up can also make a one-page progress report about you — your nickname, your year or grade, the topics you've learned and a few things to practise together. It is made on their phone, we never get a copy, and it only goes to someone else if your grown-up chooses to send it.

Is my score used against me?

No. Your progress only ever goes up as you learn — we never take points away to punish a wrong answer. Getting something wrong just means we show you how it works so you can get it next time. If you'd rather not see a score at all, your grown-up can turn it off.

Something wrong with a question?

If a question looks wrong or confusing, tap "Report a problem". That tells us to check it and fix it. Thank you — it really helps!

Want everything deleted?

Ask your parent or carer. They can delete the whole account, and everything about you goes with it, for good.

If you ever have a worry, tell a grown-up you trust, and they can contact us.

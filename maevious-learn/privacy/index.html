1. Who we are and what this covers

Maevious Learn is a free practice app for school students. Each child's profile follows one curriculum, chosen by the parent or carer: the national curriculum of England (Key Stages 1–4) or a US curriculum for grades 2–12 (built from published US standards such as the Common Core and the Next Generation Science Standards). It is published by Daralexion Ltd ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for the information described here.

Maevious Learn is a parent/carer app. The account holder is an adult. Your child uses the app through a learner profile you create inside your account — your child is never asked to create their own login, give an email address, or tell us anything about themselves.

The online schools promoted inside the app ("Online school") are operated by separate companies: Maevious Ltd (the UK school, maevious.com) and Maevious US, Inc. (the US school, maevious.us). Each is a separate data controller with its own privacy notice. Which school the app shows depends only on the curriculum you chose for each child's profile (England, US, or both when they differ); we do not share anything about you or your child with either school, and using this app does not sign you up to any marketing from either of them.

We do not collect your child's email address, date of birth, exact age, location, photograph, voice, contacts, or any way to contact your child. There is no chat, no messaging, no public profile and no way for a child to post or share anything. There are no adverts in the app.

On your device only

Some information stays on the phone or tablet and is never sent to us: the parent PIN (kept in the device's secure storage), practice reminders (scheduled by the device itself — we hold no push-notification address for your device), display preferences such as daily goals and hiding scores, and read-aloud, which uses the device's own text-to-speech. The app also keeps a copy of your family's profiles and progress on the device so that practice works offline; signing out or deleting your account removes that copy.

Usage analytics

The app does not currently send any usage analytics. It contains support for privacy-preserving analytics (PostHog, hosted in the EU), but this is switched off in the published app. If we ever switch it on, we will update this policy first. When on, it would be limited to a fixed list of events (such as "lesson completed"), tied to a random account identifier and never to your email, your name, your child's nickname, or any free text.

Logs kept by our providers

Like any online service, our hosting and email providers keep technical logs for security and reliability — for example, sign-in events and the network (IP) address a request came from, and whether an email was delivered.

3. Our lawful bases (UK GDPR)

A fuller table is in our lawful-basis map. In summary:

  • Contract / provision of the service — creating your account, storing learner profiles and progress, and sending the account emails and the weekly summary.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app secure, and fixing faulty content you report. We have balanced these against your and your child's interests and kept them narrow.
  • Consent — any Maevious Ltd school marketing is done only with a separate, active opt-in collected by Maevious Ltd on its own website, never inside this app. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

We never market to children. Information about the school is shown to parents only, behind the parent PIN, and is clearly labelled.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your account and your learner profiles for as long as your account exists.
  • Remove a child's profile at any time (edit the profile → "Remove this profile"). This deletes the profile and all of that child's progress, streak, review and mistake records from our database and from the device.
  • Delete your whole account at any time inside the app (Settings → parent check → Delete account). This deletes your sign-in and, through our database, every learner profile, all progress, streak, review and mistake records, your messages to us and your problem reports. It also clears the app's copy on the device.
  • Copies can remain for a short time in our providers' encrypted backups and in the technical logs described in section 2, until those are overwritten on the providers' own schedules.
  • Full detail is in our retention and deletion policy.

6. Your rights (UK and EU)

Under UK GDPR you (and, where appropriate, your child) have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and port your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Use the in-app deletion described above, or email info@maevious.com for anything else.

7. Security

  • The app reaches our database only through an interface secured by row-level security: an account can only ever reach its own family's data, enforced by the database itself.
  • Administrative keys never ship in the app.
  • All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The parent area, the profile settings and account deletion sit behind a parent PIN or an adult check.

8. Children's Code (UK)

We have designed the app to the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code: high privacy by default, data minimisation, no nudge techniques or dark patterns, no profiling of children for advertising, no location data at all, child-friendly explanations, and no detrimental use of children's data. This is documented in our internal Data Protection Impact Assessment (available on request).

9. For parents in the United States

This section applies to families using the app in the United States, including children under 13.

How a child comes to use the app. Only an adult can create an account: the account holder signs up with their own email address and password and confirms that they are the child's parent or carer. A child's learner profile can only be created inside that account, by the account holder. Children never sign up, log in, or enter an email address, and the app never asks a child for personal information.

What we collect from or about your child, how we use it, and who sees it are described in sections 2, 4 and 5. In short: a nickname you choose, the curriculum and grade you choose, an avatar, and the record of their practice — used only to run the app and to show you their progress. We do not show advertising, do not use your child's information for advertising or to build advertising profiles, and do not sell it or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Your choices as a parent. You can at any time:

  • Review your child's information: the parent area of the app shows each child's profile and practice progress, and you can email us for a copy of everything we hold about your child.
  • Delete it: remove your child's profile in the app (section 5), delete your whole account, or email us and we will delete it for you.
  • Refuse further collection: removing your child's profile, or deleting your account, stops any further collection about your child. You can also email us.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Maevious Learn is a free practice app for students following the national curriculum of England. It is published by Daralexion Ltd ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the information described here.

Maevious Learn is a parent/carer app. The account holder is an adult. Your child uses the app through a learner profile you create inside your account — your child is never asked to create their own login, and we deliberately hold as little information about your child as possible.

The online school promoted inside the app ("Our School") is operated by a separate company, Maevious Ltd, which is a separate data controller with its own privacy notice. Signing your child up to practise in this app does not sign you up to any Maevious Ltd marketing.

We practise data minimisation: we collect the least we can to run the app.

About your child (the learner profile)

We do not collect your child's email, date of birth, precise age, location, photograph, or any way to contact your child. There is no chat, no messaging, and no user-generated content anywhere a child can reach.

Usage analytics (privacy-preserving)

We use PostHog, hosted in the EU, to understand how the app is used at an aggregate level (for example, how many practice sessions are completed). This is configured to be privacy-preserving:

  • events are tied to a random account identifier, never your email or name;
  • we never send a child's nickname, or any free text, or anything that could identify a child;
  • there is no automatic screen or tap capture, no session replay, and no advertising or behavioural tracking SDK anywhere in the app.

3. Our lawful bases (UK GDPR)

A fuller table is in our lawful-basis map. In summary:

  • Contract / provision of the service — creating your account, storing learner profiles and progress, and sending you the weekly service summary.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app secure, fixing faulty content you report, and privacy-preserving analytics to improve the app. We have balanced these against your and your child's interests and kept them narrow.
  • Consent — any Maevious Ltd school marketing is done only with separate, active opt-in collected by Maevious Ltd on its own website, never inside this app. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Marketing to children is never done. Promotion of the school is parent-facing, sits behind an adult check, is clearly labelled as advertising, and is never pushed to a child's device.

International transfers. Our database, authentication and analytics are EU-hosted. Where a processor is outside the UK/EEA (e.g. Resend in the US, or Anthropic in the US for content generation), the transfer is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / IDTA (or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses), as incorporated into that processor's data-processing agreement.

Daralexion Ltd and Maevious Ltd are separate controllers; any data passing between them (for example, confirming that a referral converted) is governed by a data-sharing agreement and you receive the normal privacy notice from whichever company you interact with.

5. How long we keep it, and deleting your data

  • We keep your account and your child's learner profiles for as long as your account is active.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app at any time (Settings → after an adult check → Delete account). This permanently and irreversibly removes your account, every learner profile, and all progress, streak, report and concern records, cascading through our database.
  • Full detail is in our retention and deletion policy.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you (and, where appropriate, your child) have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and port your personal data, and to complain to the ICO. To exercise any right, use the in-app deletion for erasure, or contact us at support@maevious.com. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Security

  • The app talks to our database only through a restricted, per-user API secured by row-level security — an account can only ever reach its own family's data, enforced by the database itself.
  • Administrative database keys never ship in the app.
  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Children's Code

We have designed the app to the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code: high privacy by default, data minimisation, no nudge techniques or dark patterns, no behavioural profiling of children, geolocation off (we don't collect it at all), transparency in child-friendly language, and no detrimental use of children's data. This is documented in our internal Data Protection Impact Assessment (available on request).

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, tell you in the app or by email.
